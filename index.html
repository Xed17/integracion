Deducción de la Referencia Analítica Exacta

$$\int_0^{12} \left[ 10 + 5\sin\left(\frac{\pi t}{4}\right) + e^{0.15t} \right] dt = \left[ 10t - \frac{20}{\pi}\cos\left(\frac{\pi t}{4}\right) + \frac{e^{0.15t}}{0.15} \right]_0^{12}$$ $$\begin{aligned} F(12) &= 10(12) - \frac{20}{\pi}\cos(3\pi) + \frac{e^{0.15(12)}}{0.15} = 120 + \frac{20}{\pi} + \frac{e^{1.8}}{0.15} \\ F(0) &= 10(0) - \frac{20}{\pi}\cos(0) + \frac{e^0}{0.15} = -\frac{20}{\pi} + \frac{1}{0.15} \\ D_{\text{ref}} &= F(12) - F(0) = 120 + \frac{40}{\pi} + \frac{e^{1.8} - 1}{0.15} \approx 166.396712\text{ Mb} \end{aligned}$$
